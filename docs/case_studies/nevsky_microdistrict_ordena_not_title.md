# Case Study — МКР «Невский»: the showcase where the population transfer is the point, and the title never comes

**Accession:** MUP-CS-008 · Modality M7 — showcase/military reconstruction · [REGISTRY.md](REGISTRY.md)

**The Russian Ministry of Defence built a 115-hectare, ~1,880-apartment
microdistrict on ул. Куприна in occupied Mariupol, fenced it, put a
checkpoint on the gate, and moved displaced Mariupol families in by
administrative warrant. Vladimir Putin toured it in March 2023 and called it
no worse than "a good Moscow district." It is the most-photographed act of
"reconstruction" in the city — and, on the documents, one of the least
title-bearing. The state media that promised the new residents ownership
("будет в собственности") described, in the same breath, a housing warrant
(ордер) handed out at the local administration's discretion behind a pass
gate. Independent reporting says the units are held on социальный наём — a
revocable municipal tenancy that cannot be sold, gifted, or inherited. This
is the seizure pipeline's inverse image: everywhere else in this project the
loss is loud and the replacement is thin; here the replacement is the loudest
object in the city and the loss is hidden inside the fine print of a warrant.**

This is a different modality from the four already documented. `nakhimova_82_
chernomorsky_1b.md` and `troianda_m_demolition_challenge.md` track buildings
taken *from* their owners. `mass_registry_to_resale.md` tracks intact flats
stripped through the "ownerless" registry and resold on the open market.
Нахимова 82 ends with apartments **sold** to incomers under the 2%
mortgage. МКР «Невский» is the other end of the same policy: apartments
**given** — as spectacle, on camera, to a curated set of displaced Ukrainians
— but given as contingent possession, not restored title, by the same state
that made the destruction and the displacement. The population-transfer fact
(Rome Statute art. 8(2)(b)(viii)) is normally the hardest thing this project
has to argue from documents. Here the occupier argues it for us, with a
ribbon-cutting.

## 1. Property and site profile

МКР «Невский» — the **microdistrict**, not to be confused with a separate,
smaller commercial ЖК of the same name next door (see §6) — occupies roughly
**115 hectares** in the Октябрьский / Zhovtnevyi (Жовтневый) district of
Mariupol, in the area of **ул. Куприна and пр. Ленина** ([vskmo.ru](https://vskmo.ru/2023/12/08/doma-gde-poselilos-schaste/);
[TASS](https://tass.ru/obschestvo/20450945), state wire, bot-blocked to
scripted fetch but live in-browser). The name is part of a deliberate
Russification of Mariupol's toponymy — the occupation's new districts are
branded «Невский», «Ленинградский», «Нахимовский» after Russian cities and
naval figures, the connective "toponymy" rung (H) of the
[dispossession pipeline](../exhibits/dispossession-pipeline.html).

**Pre-war site history (open).** No pre-2022 satellite imagery, cadastral
record, or Ukrainian-era listing for the specific ул. Куприна parcels was
recovered for this write-up. Whether the microdistrict stands on cleared
Ukrainian-era housing (the Нахимова-82 address-laundering pattern) or on
previously undeveloped land at the city's north-western edge is **not
resolved** and should not be asserted either way. What is documented is that a
Moscow-registered contractor, ООО «Геострой-2010», was pouring foundations for
new apartment blocks on this same street as early as September 2022
(@mariupol_nash msg 16377, 26.10.2022) — i.e. construction here began within
months of the siege, unusually early for Mariupol reconstruction.

## 2. The builder: the Russian Ministry of Defence, in its own name

МКР «Невский» was built directly by **ППК «Военно-строительная компания»**
(VSK — the "Military Construction Company"), a Russian **public-law company
under the founding authority of the Ministry of Defence**. VSK is not a
commercial "специализированный застройщик"; it builds "объекты военной и
социальной инфраструктуры" as a state instrument. That single fact is why
this development is structurally invisible to this project's normal tracking:
it has **no entry in the ЕИСЖС / наш.дом.рф new-build registry**
(`data/parsed/eisghs_mariupol_objects.jsonl`, checked exhaustively by name and
by street), because that registry only tracks commercial долевое-строительство.
The most-showcased housing in the city sits entirely outside the
commercial-developer pipeline this project was built to follow.

VSK was itself created by a named federal instrument: **Указ Президента
Российской Федерации от 18 октября 2019 г. № 504 «О создании публично-правовой
компании "Военно-строительная компания"»**, signed by Vladimir Putin
([kremlin.ru](http://kremlin.ru/acts/bank/44754)). Указ №504 authorises VSK
generically to build nationwide; **no site-specific instrument** — no DNR
land-grant, no ГКО постановление naming the ул. Куприна parcel and assigning
it to VSK — was located. This is the answer to research task N1, and it is a
*negative* answer that is itself the finding: unlike every commercial
developer this project tracks (each with a 93-prefix DNR-registered SPV and a
Пушилин land-grant decree), VSK operates as a **state-direct contractor with
no DNR-local corporate vehicle at all** — federal institution-building on
occupied land, keyed to no local title transfer this project can capture.

VSK's own site states the scale in its own triumphal voice — the occupier
documenting the act for us ([vskmo.ru](https://vskmo.ru/2023/12/08/doma-gde-poselilos-schaste/);
[vskmo.ru, Oct 2022](https://vskmo.ru/2022/10/20/novyj-zhk-v-mariupole-teper-vysotki/)):

| Phase | Duration | Buildings | Apartments |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | **181 days** | 12 monolithic 5-storey | **1,011** |
| 2023 | **273 days** | 6 nine-storey | **869** |
| **Total** | — | **18 residential** + school (1,100 pupils) + kindergarten (150) | **≈1,880** |

This **reconciles the scale conflict** the outsourced research pass could not
resolve: the "12 пятиэтажек / 1,011 квартир" figure (VSK, Dossier Center) and
the "6 девятиэтажек" figure (mariupol-news.ru, April 2024) are not
contradictory accounts of one building set — they are the **two build phases**
of the same microdistrict. The verb VSK uses for the handover is its own
self-incrimination: the 2023 blocks were, in its words, ones the military
builders «**подарили** мариупольцам» — *gifted* to the residents of the city
they had just finished besieging.

## 3. The beneficiary network: VSK → Олимпситистрой → Иванов

Beneath the VSK decree runs a documented three-tier delivery chain already on
this project's stakeholder spine (`memory/vskmo_olimpsitistroy_chain_2026-06-20.md`;
[stakeholder network](../exhibits/stakeholder-network.html)). Independent
investigation by the **Dossier Center** ([dossier.center](https://dossier.center/hus-ivanov/))
found that the actual construction was carried out by subcontractor **ООО
«Олимпситистрой»** (ИНН 7719585979, ОГРН 1067746433204, Moscow), which it
states «построила в Мариуполе ЖК "Невский"», tied to then–Deputy Defence
Minister **Тимур Иванов**, who had personally lobbied VSK into existence in
2019–2020. Иванов was arrested in April 2024 and **convicted of corruption in
Russia itself** in 2025; Olimpsitistroy's president Александр Фомин was
detained alongside him. The chain is corroborated by
[Novaya Gazeta Europe](https://novayagazeta.eu/articles/2024/02/22/dostupnoe-zakhvachennoe-zhile),
Newizv, and the Moscow Times, and VSK's Mariupol turnover reportedly rose by
roughly 130 billion roubles after the full-scale invasion.

confirmation of exactly the role its own site advertises.

## 4. The allocation mechanism — the ордер, the pass gate, and the title that never comes

This is the case's centre of gravity. How, legally, does a displaced Mariupol
family "receive" one of these apartments?

**By ордер — a Soviet-era housing warrant — issued at the local
administration's discretion, behind a checkpoint.** State-aligned outlet
Аргументы и Факты described the move-in mechanism plainly in a piece whose own
headline is the euphemism autopsy this project exists to perform —
**«Новоселье по пропуску»**, *a housewarming by security pass*
([aif.ru](https://aif.ru/society/people/novosele_po_propusku_kak_pogorelcy_v_mariupole_poluchayut_novye_kvartiry),
01.12.2022):

> «…огорожен забором, здесь дежурит охрана… действует пропускной режим.»
> "…fenced off, guards on duty… a pass regime is in effect."

Who gets in is decided not by a court, not by a registry, but by the occupation
administration: «Кто из обездоленных мариупольцев заедет в новое жилье —
распределяют в местной администрации» ("which of the dispossessed Mariupol
residents moves into the new housing is allocated at the local
administration"). As of that report, 858 of the first 1,011 flats were handed
over and 460 occupied.

And here is the fracture. The same state outlet asserted, in the same article,
that the flats were the residents' *property*:

> «Это не маневренный фонд, и квартира будет в собственности новосёлов.»
> "This is not the transit-housing fund — the apartment **will be** the new
> residents' property."

Read it in the future tense the occupier chose: *будет* — will be. It is a
promise, not a deed. Independent reporting describes what was actually
conferred: not ownership but **договор социального найма** — a revocable
municipal social-tenancy, under which the occupant may live in the flat but
**cannot sell, gift, or bequeath it**; ownership arrives, if ever, only through
a later privatisation ([Север.Реалии / RFE-RL](https://www.severreal.org/a/kak-v-mariupole-poluchayut-novye-kvartiry-/32367722.html);
[Verstka](https://verstka.media/kak-vlasti-priznayut-beshoznym-zhile-na-okkupirovannyh-territoriyah);
[Novaya-Europe](https://novayagazeta.eu/articles/2024/02/22/dostupnoe-zakhvachennoe-zhile)).
The ордер conveys possession; the социальный наём conditions it; the собственность
is deferred to a privatisation step that requires exactly the thing the same
administration spent months withholding — a working property register. In 2024,
by the DNR Head's Administration's own admission, ГКО постановление **№341**
caused a "техническая накладка" that **suspended property-rights registration
across the DNR for months**, remedied only by Пушилин Указ **№307**
(@mariupol_nash msgs 82512 / 85263 / 88180). The promised title runs through a
gate the state can close at will.

So the legal status the outsourced research flagged as the single most
important open question resolves not to a clean "temporary" or "permanent" but
to a **structured deferral**: possession now, by administrative grace, inside a
fence; ownership later, maybe, subject to a registry the occupier controls and
has already shown it will freeze. For a displaced owner whose *actual*
Ukrainian title to a *destroyed* home is recognised by no occupation
instrument, the ордер is not restitution. It is re-tenanting the dispossessed
into the occupier's housing stock — and calling it a gift.

## 5. The showcase: Turchak's ribbon, Putin's tour, and the heckle the Kremlin cut

МКР «Невский» is not merely built; it is *performed*. The performance is itself
evidence — of intent, and of the audience the occupier was addressing.

**The launch (23 July 2022).** United Russia's own website
([er.ru](https://er.ru/activity/news/edinaya-rossiya-pomozhet-s-zhilyom-mnogodetnoj-seme-iz-mariupolya-chej-dom-razrushili-vsu))
records party General Council Secretary **Andrei Turchak** visiting the
**Ponomaryov family** — a mother and, per the party's account, seven children,
two of them adopted — whose home had been destroyed, and promising: «пока семье
предоставят временное жилье, а осенью она переедет в квартиру в новом
микрорайоне Мариуполя» ("for now the family will be given temporary housing,
and in the autumn will move to an apartment in Mariupol's new microdistrict").
The autumn move-in is the 26 September 2022 event this project first found in
the @mariupol_nash corpus (msg 13817), which named the mother "Nina," gave her
"five children," and placed her in apartment No. 87 on ул. Куприна — the channel
repost's figures differ from the party's own (Nadezhda / seven / two adopted);
the official er.ru account is the primary record. Either way, the pairing is
the textbook art. 8(2)(b)(viii) messaging shape: a specific named displaced
family, a destroyed home, and a senior official of the occupying power's ruling
party handing over the replacement on camera.

**The tour (18–19 March 2023).** Vladimir Putin visited Mariupol at night,
driven through the districts by Deputy PM Marat Khusnullin, and walked МКР
«Невский» specifically, telling residents the complex was no worse than "a good
Moscow or Petersburg quarter" ([Rossiyskaya Gazeta](https://rg.ru/2023/03/21/kakim-vladimir-putin-uvidel-mariupol-a-mariupol-prezidenta-reportazh-iz-rozhdaiushchegosia-zanovo-goroda.html);
[mariupol-news.ru](https://mariupol-news.ru/society/2023/03/19/34893.html), which
captions its footage «Тот самый микрорайон… который сегодня посетил Путин»). It
is, on the available record, the single most politically significant property
site this project has documented: the head of the occupying state personally
inspecting the housing his ministry built on the city it destroyed.

And the showcase cracked on its own stage. During the walkabout, a resident's
voice is heard on the Kremlin's *own* published video shouting **«Это всё
неправда! Это всё напоказ!»** — *"It's all fake! It's all for show!"* — after
which the episode was quietly edited out of the official footage
([Meduza](https://meduza.io/news/2023/03/20/vo-vremya-vstrechi-putina-s-zhitelyami-mariupolya-kto-to-kriknul-eto-vse-nepravda-eto-vse-napokaz-etot-moment-popal-na-video-kremlya)).
The occupier filmed its own rebuttal and then reached for the scissors. This
project does not have to characterise the propaganda; the propagandist
characterised it and then tried to unsay it.

## 6. The name clash: the *other* «Невский» (commercial)

The @mariupol_nash channel had to publish its own correction (msg 64629,
14.01.2024) distinguishing МКР «Невский» from a **separate, smaller, two-
building commercial ЖК «Невский»** next to it, built «за личные средства» by
Moscow's **ООО «ОборонСпецСтрой»** — which is itself already a node in the same
VSK→Олимпситистрой→Оборонспецстрой→Иванов chain. The two "Nevskys" are best
read not as a coincidence but as two arms of one patronage network: the
military-built microdistrict handed out by warrant, and a commercial namesake
sold for cash, sharing a brand, a block, and a beneficiary chain. The
commercial ЖК is out of scope for the population-transfer analysis below and is
noted here only to prevent conflation.

## 7. Legal analysis

**Rome Statute art. 8(2)(b)(viii) — transfer of the occupier's own population,
and the treatment of the occupied.** МКР «Невский» evidences both limbs. The
allocation priority documented for occupied-territory housing — силовики,
military, officials, teachers, doctors (the ФКЗ-4 recipient class; @mariupol_nash
msg 156241) — settles the occupier's chosen personnel into state-built stock,
while the displaced original population is admitted only by revocable warrant.
The showcase messaging (Turchak, Putin, the "gift") is the demographic
re-engineering stated aloud. Art. **8(2)(a)(iv)** (appropriation of property not
justified by military necessity) attaches to the underlying land and to any
pre-war housing cleared to build it (see the open §1 question).

**RD4U — Register of Damage for Ukraine.** For the original owners whose
destroyed homes were "replaced" here, the operative category remains **A3.6**
(loss of access to property in occupied territory): an ордер into a social-
tenancy flat does not restore, and is not, their lost title — it is contingent
possession granted by the dispossessing authority. Where a specific pre-war
Ukrainian building is later confirmed to have stood on the microdistrict
footprint, **A3.1** (destruction of residential property) attaches as well.

**The core finding.** МКР «Невский» is the visible pole of the same axis as the
"ownerless" registry and the Нахимова-82 rebuild-and-resell: displaced owners
are separated from title, and the occupier decides who lives where. The
registry does it silently, by declaring a home ничьё. The microdistrict does it
loudly, by declaring a warrant a gift. The paperwork points the same direction;
only the volume differs.
